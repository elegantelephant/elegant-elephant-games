# Privacy Policy for Celebrate The Date

**Effective date:** November 24, 2026

Celebrate The Date ("the app") is built to keep the personal information you enter — your dates, names, and notes — private, on your device, and out of our hands. This policy explains, plainly, what that means, and discloses the small amount of anonymous usage information we do collect, the advertising shown in the app, and the optional in-app purchases available.

## Summary

The dates, names, and descriptions you enter into Celebrate The Date never leave your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store them remotely, and we never sell or share them. The app does collect limited, anonymous, category-level usage information (described below), displays third-party advertising, and offers two optional in-app purchases.

## Information We Collect

**Your entries — none of it.** Celebrate The Date does not collect, transmit, or have any access to the dates, names, or free-text descriptions you enter. That information is stored only in an encrypted database on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

**Anonymous usage analytics — yes, limited to category-level signals.** To understand how people use the app, we collect:

- Which general *category* of entry you create (for example: a birthday, a marriage, a job, a pet) and which *relationship* category it's tagged with (for example: self, spouse, child) — never the specific date, name, or description attached to it.
- Which notification/celebration-category settings you turn on or off.

This information is associated with an anonymous identifier tied to your installation of the app, so we can see patterns in how a given installation is used over time. It is **never** linked to your name, email address, or any other information that could identify you personally, and it cannot be used to reconstruct any specific entry you've made — we never see the actual date, name, or text you typed.

## Advertising

The app displays banner advertising served by a third-party advertising network. That network may use its own identifiers and data practices to select and measure ads, governed by its own privacy policy, separate from this one. Ad content does not have access to the dates, names, or descriptions you've entered in the app.

## In-App Purchases

The app offers two optional, voluntary in-app purchases, both handled entirely through Google Play's billing system (we never receive or process your payment information ourselves):

- **Ad Removal ($1, one-time):** Permanently disables banner advertising for your installation of the app.
- **Tip Jar:** A one-time purchase, at one of several preset amounts, to support the app's development. A tip is purely voluntary and unlocks no additional content or features.

Purchase transactions are processed by Google Play under its own privacy policy. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed (so we can disable ads or show a thank-you) — never your payment details.

## Information You Enter

When you use the app, you may enter dates and descriptions related to yourself and people in your life (such as birthdays, anniversaries, or other meaningful dates). This information:

- Is stored only in an encrypted local database on your device.
- Is never transmitted to us, to any third party, or to any server of any kind.
- Is never used for advertising targeting or analytics — only the category-level signals described above are collected, and they contain none of this content.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share the anonymous usage analytics described above except as needed to operate and measure the app (for example, with an analytics service provider acting on our behalf). The advertising network that serves ads in the app operates under its own separate privacy policy and may collect its own data for ad delivery and measurement, independent of anything we collect. Purchase processing is handled by Google Play under its own separate privacy policy, as described above.

## Data Storage & Security

All of your entered data is stored locally on your device in an encrypted database. If you export a backup of your data, that backup file is also encrypted, protected by a passphrase that only you set and only you know. We have no way to access, recover, or reset that passphrase — if it's lost, the backup cannot be decrypted by anyone, including us.

## Children's Privacy

Celebrate The Date does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. The app may be used by adults to track meaningful dates for people in their lives, including their children, but the dates, names, and descriptions entered never leave the user's own device. The anonymous usage analytics described above contain no information capable of identifying any individual, child or adult.

## Your Control Over Your Data

Your entries remain entirely in your control: you can delete individual entries, clear all app data, or uninstall the app at any time, which permanently removes all locally stored information from your device. Uninstalling the app also stops any further anonymous usage analytics from being sent, since that identifier is tied to the installation itself.
